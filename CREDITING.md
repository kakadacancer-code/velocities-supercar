# Photography attribution

AI-generated editorial illustrations in `public/images/` depict the ten models in the collection. The credits below apply to the documentary photography.

VELOCITY loads properly identified car photographs hosted by Wikimedia Commons, from their original pages using `Special:FilePath` with width parameters. Photos may appear cropped and darkened in the website via CSS. Photographers and source links are included here. The `/credits` route is the user-facing attribution page.

IMPORTANT: Check the license, author and attribution information on the live source page before commercial use. Some license details are listed in the app as preliminary credits for demonstration purposes. **CC BY-SA** may have additional share-alike conditions if you distribute adapted image files. This website is not affiliated with the manufacturers pictured.
